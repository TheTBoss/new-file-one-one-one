Business 2026 – present

Clarifin

A UK digital financial templates business: working Excel models sold to people who need one but are not modellers. Live since September 2026 with 13 templates shipped.

  • Excel
  • Cloudflare Workers
  • R2
  • Stripe
  • Resend
Details

What it is

Clarifin sells ready-built financial models — three-statement, DCF and LBO templates, and smaller operational tools — to founders, analysts and small businesses who need a defensible model without building one from a blank sheet.

What I built

All of it. Every model in the catalogue is one I wrote and tested. The storefront runs on Cloudflare Workers with files served from R2, checkout through Stripe, and automated delivery by email through Resend. No third-party storefront platform sits in the middle.

What it taught me

Pricing is harder than modelling. So is writing instructions that a non-specialist can follow without a call.

Research Multi-week series

Hollywood Bowl Group — LBO series

A full leveraged buyout build on a listed UK operator: operating model, KPI dashboard and returns analysis, published week by week.

  • Excel
  • LBO
  • Operating model
  • Returns analysis
Details

The question

Whether centre-level cash generation at a UK ten-pin bowling operator supports a sponsor case, and what has to be true about footfall and spend per game for the returns to work.

The build

An operating model driven from centre count and unit economics rather than top-down growth rates, a KPI dashboard to keep the drivers visible, then a debt schedule and returns analysis with the entry and exit multiples flexed.

Why it was worth doing

Building it over several weeks in public meant every assumption had to be defensible to anyone reading along.

Research Independent research

Microsoft — three-scenario DCF

Bear, base and bull cases with sensitivity analysis on WACC and terminal growth, isolating how much of the valuation rests on the terminal assumption.

  • Excel
  • DCF
  • Sensitivity analysis
Details

The approach

Three explicitly separated scenarios rather than one forecast with a confidence interval bolted on, so the difference between cases is visible in the operating assumptions instead of buried in a growth rate.

The finding worth stating

A two-way sensitivity on WACC against terminal growth shows how much of the implied value is carried by the terminal period — the part of a DCF most likely to be quietly doing the work.
