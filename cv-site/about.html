01 Introduction

About me

I study finance and mathematics, build things that use both, and spend a good part of my life training cadets.

The degree was a deliberate pairing. Finance on its own teaches you the vocabulary; the mathematics half forces you to be honest about what a number actually rests on. That shows up in how I model: assumptions on the face of the sheet, sensitivities run before conclusions, and no output I cannot walk somebody through.

Outside the degree I run Clarifin, a small business selling financial templates to people who need a working model but are not modellers. I built all of it myself — the spreadsheets, the site, the payment and delivery plumbing — which taught me more about operating a business than any module has.

The constant across eleven years has been the Army Cadet Force. I joined as a cadet and now command a company as an adult volunteer. It is where I learned to plan properly, to brief clearly, and to make the call when a decision has a safety consequence attached.
